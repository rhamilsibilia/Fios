# FIOS Architecture

> Version 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

FIOS is the authoritative source of executive intelligence.

Its architecture exists to preserve, organize, connect, retrieve, and compound the intellectual capital of Rhamil Sibilia across businesses, projects, conversations, systems, codebases, research, decisions, relationships, and future technologies.

FIOS is not designed around any single artificial intelligence model, application, vendor, or interface.

Every AI, agent, application, and workflow is a consumer of FIOS.

FIOS remains the source.
