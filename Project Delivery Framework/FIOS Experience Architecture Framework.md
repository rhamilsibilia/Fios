# FIOS Experience Architecture Framework

A web platform is not primarily a collection of sections.

It is an experience designed to move a visitor from one state of
understanding, emotion, or intention to another.

Before visual design begins, every project must establish:

1. Who the business is.
2. Who the visitor is.
3. What the visitor is experiencing when they arrive.
4. What the business wants them to understand.
5. What transformation the experience should create.
6. What story expresses that transformation.
7. What journey allows the visitor to experience that story.
8. What proof makes the story credible.
9. What emotional progression supports the journey.
10. What action naturally follows the experience.

DESIGN FOLLOWS CONTEXT.

The stronger the context, the more intentional, efficient,
and differentiated the creative process becomes.
